Reading the charts

K-lines, moving averages and indicators — what you see and what it means.

K-lines (daily / weekly / monthly)

Each bar shows open, high, low and close for that period. Switch timeframes to see short-term rhythm or long-term structure.

Moving averages (overlay)

Orange line · shorter-term rhythm Blue line · medium-term structure Green line · long-term trend Dashed short MA · optional toggle for earlier crossovers

MACD · RSI · Volume

MACD and RSI in the subchart show momentum and overbought/oversold conditions; volume bars show activity.

Indicators describe the current picture — they don't predict the future. Read them together with price action.

FAQ

How do I search a stock, ETF or index?

Type a ticker such as AAPL, SPY or ^GSPC into the search box and press Search. Quotes, K-lines and indicators load automatically.

What indicators are available?

Daily, weekly and monthly K-lines with MA, MACD, RSI and volume.

What does the AI commentary tell me?

A plain-language technical snapshot of a symbol: trend, momentum, volume and watch points. It is educational, not investment advice.

How do I subscribe to Markets Pro?

Pro is $9.9/week, $24.9/month or $199/year (33% off), paid with PayPal. Activate it inside Markets or from your Account.

Why is the data delayed by 15 minutes?

We use free delayed market feeds. Sources are credited on every page; content is for educational purposes only.

Do I need a separate account for Markets?

No — sign in with the same AI24X account. Log in from the Markets app and manage billing from your Account.

Searching a symbol returns nothing?

Check the ticker spelling and use the exchange format (e.g. ^GSPC for the S&P 500). Some symbols on free feeds may be temporarily unavailable.

Delisted or renamed symbols

Delisted companies may no longer be available. Try the new ticker after a rename.

Why do daily, weekly and monthly look different?

Different timeframes show different scales, so markers and indicators naturally differ. Don't compare them directly.

Why do numbers differ from other apps?

Different platforms use different feeds and compilation methods. Please rely on the data shown on this site.

Can I trade based on the AI commentary?

No. Commentary, indicators and charts are educational references only. Markets involve risk; make your own decisions.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Sign in to your Account (same sign-in as Markets) to manage your subscription. Cancel anytime; access continues until the paid period ends.
