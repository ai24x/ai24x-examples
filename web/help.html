FAQ

What model should I send?

Use flash, pro, ultra, or auto. Members can also pick China models by name.

International payment

Pricing → Console → PayPal (USD). Domestic CNY: WeChat Pay / Alipay in Console.

401 / unauthorized

Check that your API key is correct, active, and your account is available.

Out of balance?

Top up in Console. If free shared mode is on, you may continue on shared within daily caps.

429 / rate limited

Slow down or upgrade plan.

Referrals

Share your invite code or link—see the Referrals page.

还是不行？

优先用控制台「转人工」提交工单并查看进度；紧急账单问题可用注册邮箱说明问题与大致时间。
