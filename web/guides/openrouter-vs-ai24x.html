What they share

  • One endpoint for many models — no per-provider accounts.
  • OpenAI protocol, so existing SDKs and tools keep working.
  • Credit-based usage, so you control spend.

Where AI24X differs

  • Price clarity: a published starting price ($0.35/M flash) and transparent per-request breakdowns — no routing-fee surprises.
  • Smart routing: AI24X evaluates channel price, speed and stability per request, and fails over automatically if an upstream degrades.
  • PayPal checkout: instant payment for international users without a credit card.
  • Off-peak discounts: automatic cheaper rates in off-peak hours (Beijing time) for cost-sensitive workloads.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI24X OpenAI-compatible?
Yes. Use POST /v1/chat/completions with Bearer auth; streaming (SSE) is supported. Most clients only need a base_url change.

Can I keep my existing model names?
Common OpenAI-compatible model names are mapped automatically; short aliases like flash and pro also work.

What happens if an upstream is slow?
AI24X routes around it automatically — that's the point of smart routing.
