PayPal checkout (USD)

AI24X sells prepaid Token plans for our multi-model AI API. International buyers pay with PayPal; tokens credit to the account after capture.

Digital goods only — no physical shipping. China (CNY) uses WeChat / Alipay in Console.

How to pay with PayPal

  1. Open Pricing and choose a Token plan.
  2. Sign in (or register) — Checkout credits the logged-in wallet.
  3. In Console, open Token plans and click PayPal.
  4. On PayPal.com, sign in to your PayPal account (guest card checkout may be limited) and complete payment; you return to Console and tokens are credited.
