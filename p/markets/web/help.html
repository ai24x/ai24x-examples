Get started in three steps

From sign-in to reading the chart.

1. Sign in or register

One AI24X account works for Markets and Account Hub. Start free — no card required.

2. Search a symbol

Type a ticker like AAPL, SPY or ^GSPC and press Search. Quotes, K-lines and indicators load automatically.

3. Read the charts

Switch daily / weekly / monthly and read the AI commentary under the chart.

Tip: wait for charts to finish loading before searching again; rapid repeated searches can hit data-source rate limits.

Reading the charts

K-lines, moving averages and indicators — what you see and what it means.

K-lines (daily / weekly / monthly)

Each bar shows open, high, low and close for that period. Switch timeframes to see short-term rhythm or long-term structure.

Moving averages (overlay)

Orange · shorter-term rhythm Blue · medium-term structure Green · long-term trend Dashed short MA · optional

MACD · RSI · Volume

MACD and RSI in the subchart show momentum and overbought/oversold conditions; volume bars show activity.

Indicators describe the current picture — they don’t predict the future. Read them together with price action.

FAQ

How do I search a stock, ETF or index?

Type a ticker such as AAPL, SPY or ^GSPC into the search box and press Search.

What indicators are available?

Daily, weekly and monthly K-lines with MA, MACD, RSI and volume.

What does the AI commentary tell me?

A plain-language technical snapshot of trend, momentum, volume and watch points. Educational only — not investment advice.

How do I subscribe to Markets Pro?

Pay with PayPal inside Markets (Upgrade to Pro) or from Account Hub. Plans: $9.9/week, $24.9/month or $199/year.

Why is the data delayed by 15 minutes?

We use delayed market feeds. Sources are credited on the page; content is for educational purposes only.

Do I need a separate account for Markets?

No — use the same AI24X account. Sign in from Markets; manage billing in Account Hub when needed.

Searching a symbol returns nothing?

Check the ticker spelling (e.g. ^GSPC for the S&P 500). Some symbols on free feeds may be temporarily unavailable.

Why do daily, weekly and monthly look different?

Different timeframes use different scales, so markers and indicators naturally differ.

Can I trade based on the AI commentary?

No. Commentary, indicators and charts are educational references only. Markets involve risk; make your own decisions.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Sign in to Account Hub (same login as Markets). Cancel anytime; access continues until the paid period ends.
